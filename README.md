## Core Principles Summary for Development Team
1. **Safety Separation:** Our AI advises; human controllers authorize. The AI never controls signals or routes directly.
2. **CP-SAT for Scheduling; ML for Prediction:** Use LightGBM for durations/risks, and Google OR-Tools CP-SAT for constraint satisfaction.
3. **Shadow Block Bundling is the Win Condition:** Merging Civil, Electrical, and S&T tasks into single track closures delivers the primary operational ROI.
4. **Resilient Dynamic Replanning:** The demo will shine brightest when an unexpected train delay is introduced, and the system dynamically recalculates the schedule in under 15 seconds.
